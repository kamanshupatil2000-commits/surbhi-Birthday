Happy birthday, Dubruuuu. ❤️

I know I probably shouldn’t be saying all this today… but then again, I’m pretty sure I’ve already written something in your gift anyway. 🥹

I never claim that I’ll be the best person in your life. But I can promise you one thing — I’ll always keep trying to become a better version of myself for you. Whenever you feel that I’ve done enough, I’ll try to do even more the next time. Not because I have to prove anything, but because you’ve always been that important to me.

Your birthday was always special to me, but for the last three years, it has meant more to me than I can explain. I used to have so many plans for your birthdays… so many little things I wanted to do for you. But somewhere in my mind, there was always this fear that you might start doubting me, thinking I was doing all of this because I liked you. So I kept holding myself back.

This time, I had finally decided that I wanted to make your birthday really special. I had so many things in my head… and then Diwali came in between. 🥹

But I’ll make you one promise — someday in my life, I’ll make one of your birthdays so special that even your husband will have a hard time competing with it. 😂❤️

Maybe that’s the funny part of my one-sided story… I keep competing with myself. 😂🥹

And honestly, I don’t need your love in return anymore. I don’t want anything from you. All I’ll ever ask Ganu Bappa for is that you stay happy, genuinely happy, wherever life takes you.

Because no matter what changes, a part of me will always want the best for you.

Once again, happppppy birthday, Surbhyaaaa. ❤️🥹
Stay the same crazy, cute, soft-hearted Dubru that you are.
